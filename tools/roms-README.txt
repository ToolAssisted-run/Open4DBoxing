The Roland MT-32's ROMs go here: a control ROM and a PCM ROM, as files of any names (they are recognized by their
contents; an original MT-32's, versions 1.04 to 1.07, is the sound the game was made for; a later MT-32's or a
CM-32L's also work). They are not included: they are Roland's, and you need your own.

Then choose the MT-32 in the game's menu (F4, GENERAL, Sound: mt32) or Open4DBoxing.ini (sound = mt32), or start the
game with the driver: open4dboxing GAMEDIR n mt15.drv d

The ROMs are also looked for in your own data folder (on Linux ~/.local/share/Open4DBoxing/roms, on Windows
%APPDATA%\Open4DBoxing\roms), in the folder the OPEN4DBOXING_MT32ROMS environment variable names, and in the game's
folder. Without them, the Sound Blaster's sound plays instead.
